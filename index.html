Rāmāyaṇa & Mahābhārata

Two of the world's great epics, laid on a timeline that keeps five things apart on purpose: how the text grew, the order of events within the story, who retold it down the centuries, what the tradition holds, and the proofs people actually point to. Scholarship and belief are both here — never blurred. Nothing is presented as settled fact.
